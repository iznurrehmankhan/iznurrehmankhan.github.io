Sabt Book

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Sabt Book (“the App”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use our mobile application.

Sabt Book is a local-first expense tracker. It does not require an account, does not have a backend, and is designed to work offline. Your financial records stay on your device unless you choose to export a backup yourself.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Physical address
  • Payment card or bank login credentials

You can use the App without creating an account or providing personal information to us.

Local Data Storage

The App stores information locally on your device so it can function without a network connection. This may include:

  • Accounts, categories, expenses, and notes you enter
  • Give/Take (lending) people and entries
  • Reminders and notification schedules
  • App preferences such as language, theme, and number format
  • Optional account pictures you choose to attach

This information is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to us. If you uninstall the App, clear its data, or lose the device, that local data may be permanently lost unless you previously exported a backup.

Backups You Choose to Share

The App can export a backup file that you then share through your device’s share sheet (for example email, messaging, or cloud storage). We do not receive that file. Where it goes is entirely your choice, and it is governed by the privacy policy of the app or service you pick to receive it.

Advertising

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob, which may mediate ads from additional networks such as Meta Audience Network.

These advertising partners may automatically collect certain information to provide and improve advertising services, including:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Approximate location information

This information is collected by the advertising partners and is governed by their privacy policies, not by us.

You can review their privacy policies here:

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Meta: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

Ad Personalization

Advertisements shown in the App may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your device settings, location, and the advertising partners’ policies.

Personalized ads may use advertising identifiers to provide more relevant ads. You can manage or reset advertising identifiers through your device settings.

Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (advertising mediation and serving)
  • Meta Audience Network (advertising, when mediated through AdMob)

These services may collect information as described in their respective privacy policies.

Third-Party Data Practices

We do not control how third-party services collect or use data. Any information collected by these services is governed by their own privacy policies.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third-party providers to understand how their information is handled.

By using the application, you acknowledge that third-party advertising partners may collect certain anonymous or device-level data as described in their privacy policies.

Permissions

Depending on your platform and settings, the App may request permission to show local reminders you schedule. The App itself does not request access to contacts, your microphone, or your camera for its core expense-tracking features.

Some permissions may be used by third-party advertising services in order to deliver advertisements and maintain related functionality.

Children’s Privacy

This App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
